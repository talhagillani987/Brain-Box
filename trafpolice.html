Traffic Signs

Traffic Signs in Pakistan

Traffic signs are visual signals used to regulate, warn, and guide road users to ensure safety and smooth traffic flow. In Pakistan, traffic signs are generally divided into three main categories.

Mandatory (Regulatory) Signs

These signs tell drivers what they must or must not do. Ignoring these can lead to fines or accidents.

Stop Sign – Octagonal, red with white border. Driver must come to a complete stop.

No Entry – Red circle with a white horizontal bar. Vehicles are not allowed to enter.

Speed Limit Sign – Circular, shows maximum speed allowed (e.g., 60 km/h).

No Overtaking – Indicates overtaking other vehicles is prohibited.

No Parking – Circular sign with a red cross or bar, showing parking is not allowed.

One Way – Arrow sign showing traffic can only move in one direction.

Compulsory Turn – Blue circular sign with white arrow, showing the direction vehicles must turn.

Warning (Cautionary) Signs

These signs alert drivers to potential hazards ahead. They are usually triangular with a red border.

Sharp Curve Ahead – Warns of a bend in the road.

School Ahead – Warns drivers to slow down near schools.

Pedestrian Crossing – Warns of a crossing point for pedestrians.

Narrow Road – Warns that the road ahead becomes narrow.

Slippery Road –Warns of slippery conditions, especially during rain.

Men at Work / Road Construction – Warns of construction or repair work ahead.

Railway Crossing –Warns of an upcoming railway crossing.

Informatory (Guide) Signs

These signs give useful information to drivers, usually rectangular and blue or green.

Hospital Sign – Shows direction/distance to the nearest hospital.

Petrol Pump Sign – Indicates a fuel station nearby.

Parking Sign – Shows an area where parking is allowed.

Distance/Direction Sign – Shows distance to cities or places.
