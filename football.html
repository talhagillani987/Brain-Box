Football Information And Records

Normal Information

Football (often known as soccer in North America) is the world's most popular sport, played by two teams of 11 players. The primary objective is to score by maneuvering a round ball into the opponent’s goal using any part of the body except the hands and arms, unless you are the goalkeeper.

Basic Information

Duration: 90 minutes, split into two 45-minute halves Players: 11 per team (including goalkeeper) Field Size: 100–130 yards long, 50–100 yards wide Goal Size: 8 feet high × 8 yards wide No hands (except the goalkeeper inside the penalty area)

Penalty in Football

A penalty in football (also called soccer) is one of the most important and valuable opportunities to score a goal. A penalty kick is awarded by the referee when a defending player commits a direct free-kick offense, such as tripping, pushing, holding, handball (except by the goalkeeper in their own penalty area), kicking, or attempting to kick an opponent, inside their own penalty area while the ball is in play. When a penalty is awarded, the ball is placed on the penalty mark, which is 11 meters (12 yards) from the center of the goal. The player taking the penalty kick has a one-on-one chance against only the goalkeeper. All other players from both teams must remain outside the penalty area, outside the penalty arc, and behind the penalty mark until the ball is kicked. The goalkeeper must stand on the goal line, facing the kicker, with at least part of one foot touching or directly above the goal line until the ball is struck. After the referee blows the whistle, the kicker must kick the ball forward, and once the ball is kicked and clearly moves, it is in play. The kicker is not allowed to touch the ball again until another player has touched it. If the ball enters the goal, a goal is awarded. If the goalkeeper saves the shot, or if the ball rebounds off the goalpost or crossbar and remains in play, players may continue playing unless the referee has stopped play for another reason. Penalties are often awarded for serious fouls because they provide a very high chance of scoring, with professional players converting around 75–85% of penalty kicks. Penalties are different from penalty shootouts, which are used only after certain knockout matches end in a draw to decide the winner. In a penalty shootout, each team takes a series of penalty kicks under special rules, and the goals scored during the shootout do not count toward the match's official score. Penalty kicks require excellent accuracy, power, composure, and decision-making from the kicker, while the goalkeeper relies on quick reactions, positioning, and anticipation to make a save.

Famous Footballers

Lionel Messi (Argentina)

Lionel Messi began his professional football career with FC Barcelona in 2004 after progressing through the club's famous youth academy, La Masia. He quickly established himself as one of the world's best players with his exceptional dribbling, vision, passing, and goal-scoring ability. During his 17 seasons with Barcelona, Messi won 10 La Liga titles, 7 Copa del Rey trophies, 8 Spanish Super Cups, and 4 UEFA Champions League titles. He also became the club's all-time leading goalscorer with 672 goals in official matches. In 2021, Messi joined Paris Saint-Germain F.C. after leaving Barcelona due to financial and registration issues. During his two seasons in France, he won two Ligue 1 titles and one French Super Cup while continuing to perform at the highest level. In 2023, Messi signed with Inter Miami CF in the United States. He made an immediate impact by leading the club to its first major trophy, the Leagues Cup, and helped increase the popularity of football in North America. For the Argentina national football team, Messi made his senior debut in 2005. After several years of near misses in major tournaments, he led Argentina to victory in the 2021 Copa América, ending the country's 28-year wait for a major international trophy. In 2022, he achieved the greatest success of his career by captaining Argentina to win the 2022 FIFA World Cup, where he was awarded the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player. He also helped Argentina win the 2022 Finalissima and the 2024 Copa América. Throughout his career, Messi has won numerous league titles, domestic cups, UEFA Champions League trophies, and a record eight Ballon d'Or awards. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers in the history of the sport.

Cristiano Ronaldo(Portugal)

Cristiano Ronaldo began his professional football career in 2002 with Sporting CP, where his outstanding performances quickly attracted international attention. In 2003, he joined Manchester United, where he developed into one of the world's best players under manager Sir Alex Ferguson. During his six seasons at the club, Ronaldo won three Premier League titles, one FA Cup, two League Cups, the UEFA Champions League, and his first Ballon d'Or in 2008. In 2009, Ronaldo transferred to Real Madrid for a then world-record transfer fee. He enjoyed the most successful period of his career there, becoming the club's all-time leading goalscorer with 450 goals in 438 appearances. He won four UEFA Champions League titles, two La Liga titles, two Copa del Rey trophies, and four Ballon d'Or awards while playing for the Spanish giants. In 2018, Ronaldo joined Juventus, where he won two Serie A titles, one Coppa Italia, and two Italian Super Cups. He became one of the few players to win league titles in England, Spain, and Italy. In 2021, Ronaldo returned to Manchester United for a second spell, where he continued to score goals and reached several personal milestones before leaving the club in late 2022. In 2023, Ronaldo signed with Al Nassr FC, where he continues his professional career. He has helped increase the global popularity of football in Saudi Arabia while continuing to break goal-scoring records. For the Portugal national football team, Ronaldo made his senior debut in 2003 and became the country's all-time leading scorer and most-capped player. He captained Portugal to victory at the UEFA Euro 2016 and the 2019 UEFA Nations League Finals. Throughout his career, Ronaldo has won multiple league titles, domestic cups, UEFA Champions League trophies, and five Ballon d'Or awards, establishing himself as one of the greatest footballers in history.

Kylian Mbappé(France)

Kylian Mbappé began his professional football career with AS Monaco FC in 2015. He quickly became one of the brightest young talents in world football because of his incredible speed, dribbling skills, and finishing ability. During the 2016–17 season, he helped Monaco win the Ligue 1 title and reach the semi-finals of the UEFA Champions League. In 2017, Mbappé joined Paris Saint-Germain F.C., initially on loan before making the move permanent. At PSG, he won multiple Ligue 1 titles, French Cups, and French Super Cups. He became the club's all-time leading goalscorer and established himself as one of the best players in the world. His outstanding performances earned him several individual awards, including multiple Ligue 1 Player of the Year honors. For the France national football team, Mbappé made his senior debut in 2017. In 2018, he played a key role in helping France win the 2018 FIFA World Cup, becoming one of the youngest players ever to score in a World Cup final. In the 2022 FIFA World Cup, he won the Golden Boot after scoring eight goals, including a hat-trick in the final, although France finished as runners-up. He also helped France win the 2021 UEFA Nations League Finals. In 2024, Mbappé joined Real Madrid CF, fulfilling his long-awaited dream of playing for the Spanish giants. He continues to compete at the highest level and is widely regarded as one of the best footballers of his generation due to his pace, goal-scoring ability, and leadership qualities.
